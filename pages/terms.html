Draft terms

Service Terms

These draft terms are for prototype and planning use. They should be reviewed by counsel before public commercial use.

Non-medical service

ChinaCare Navigator provides patient navigation, translation, appointment coordination, interpreter support, and administrative document organization. It is not a medical provider.

Medical decisions

Diagnosis, prescriptions, clinical opinions, treatment plans, procedures, and medical outcomes are provided only by licensed medical institutions and medical professionals.

Fees

Platform service fees are separate from hospital, clinic, laboratory, medication, imaging, procedure, treatment, or insurance fees. Hospital fees are normally paid directly to the medical institution.

Insurance

The platform may help organize claim-supporting documents but does not guarantee reimbursement, pre-authorization, claim approval, or insurer acceptance.

Refunds

Refunds should be determined by service stage, work already completed, appointment status, interpreter scheduling, and third-party provider rules.
