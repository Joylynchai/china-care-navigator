Privacy Notice

This prototype privacy notice is for early discussion and should be reviewed by counsel before public commercial use.

Information we may collect

Contact details, preferred language, nationality, travel timing, service needs, medical records, imaging files, prescriptions, reports, insurance requirements, and communication history.

Why we process information

To provide patient navigation, translation, hospital intake preparation, appointment coordination, interpreter support, billing document organization, and follow-up administration.

Sensitive health information

Medical information is processed only for service delivery and only with customer authorization. Access should be limited to relevant case managers, translators, reviewers, and approved service partners.

Data sharing

Information may be shared with selected medical institutions, translators, interpreter companions, payment providers, or insurance-related service partners when needed to perform requested services.

Retention and deletion

Customers should be able to request deletion of records after service completion, subject to legal, accounting, dispute, and operational retention requirements.
