Positioning

Built for patients who need access, clarity, and documentation.

ChinaCare Navigator is not a hospital and does not provide medical diagnosis. It is a multilingual operating layer for international patients: intake, translation, appointment coordination, on-site interpretation, billing documents, and follow-up logistics.

Patient path

A clear workflow from intake to follow-up.

  1. 01

    Submit a request

    Patients share language, city preference, visit goal, timing, budget, and documentation needs.

  2. 02

    Prepare the file

    Case managers organize records, translations, imaging files, authorization forms, and service quotes.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the appointment

    The platform confirms hospital intake steps, schedules, visit checklists, and interpreter requirements.

  4. 04

    Support the visit

    Interpreter companions assist with registration, navigation, communication, payments, and report collection.

  5. 05

    Close the loop

    Reports, invoices, summaries, DICOM files, and insurance claim materials are organized after the visit.

Compliance stance

A coordination platform, not a medical provider.

The platform supports patient navigation, translation, appointment coordination, and administrative documentation. Diagnosis, prescriptions, clinical opinions, and treatment are provided only by licensed medical institutions and doctors.

We do

Organize intake files, translate records, coordinate appointments, assist on-site, and prepare documents.

We do not

Diagnose, prescribe, promise outcomes, rank hospitals by clinical performance, or guarantee reimbursement.
